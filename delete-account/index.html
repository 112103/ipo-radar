Delete your IPO Radar account and data

Developer: IPO Radar · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Good to know: IPO Radar keeps your data only on your own phone. We do not run a server that stores your PANs, watchlist or settings, so deleting it in the app removes it completely.

Option 1: delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open IPO Radar.
  2. Tap Profile (bottom bar).
  3. Scroll to Legal and tap Delete all my data.
  4. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

Uninstalling the app also removes everything it stored.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

Use this form if you no longer have the app or want us to confirm deletion. Your request is emailed to us and we reply within 7 days.

Form not working? Send by email instead. This opens your email app with the request already written. Or write to frontend.divine@gmail.com.

What gets deleted

  • Saved PAN numbers and their labels
  • Allotment results, watchlist, alerts and news
  • Sign-in details (name, email, profile photo link), if you signed in
  • Theme and app settings

What is kept, and for how long

We keep no app data on any server, so nothing from the app is kept. The details you send in this form (name, email, message) are used only to handle your request and are deleted within 30 days after it is completed.
