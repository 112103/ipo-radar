Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information IPO Radar ("the app", "we") handles, where it is kept and who it is shared with. In short: your PANs and settings stay on your phone, we do not run any server that stores your personal data, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

IPO Radar is an information app about Indian IPOs, made by IPO Radar. Contact: frontend.divine@gmail.com

2. Information you add

• PAN numbers and the names/labels you type for them.
• Your watchlist, alert settings and theme choice.
• Allotment results the app has checked for you.

All of this is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive it and have no copy of it.

3. Sign-in (optional)

If the app offers sign-in and you use Google or Apple to sign in, the app receives your name, email address, profile photo link and an account ID from that provider. This is kept only on your phone to show who is signed in; we do not create an account on any server. Every feature works without signing in. Google and Apple process sign-in under their own privacy policies. Signing out removes this information from the app.

4. When your PAN leaves your phone

Your PAN is sent only to the registrar of an IPO (the company that handles its allotment) and only to check allotment:
• when you tap "Check allotment" or check allotment history, or
• automatically, only if you turn on "Auto-check allotment" (the app asks for your permission first).

It is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, used only for that check, and not stored by us. Registrars are independent companies and handle the request under their own privacy policies. We never sell, rent or share your PAN with anyone else.

5. IPO data and news

IPO details, GMP and news headlines are downloaded from third-party sources. These requests do not include your PAN, name or any account information. Like any internet request, they reveal your IP address to that source.

6. No advertising

The app does not show ads and does not use your advertising ID.

7. What we do not collect

The app does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not access your location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or SMS. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

8. Permissions the app uses

• Internet: to load IPO data and news and to check allotment.
• Notifications (optional): to alert you about GMP changes, IPO dates and allotment results. You can say no or turn them off any time in Profile → Notifications or your phone's settings. Alerts are created on your phone; we do not send them from a server.

9. How long data is kept

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleting a PAN also deletes its saved allotment results. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

10. Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app has stored at any time: open Profile → Delete all my data. You can also delete single PANs, clear News, or sign out. Because we keep no copy on any server, nothing remains with us after that.

You can also ask us to delete your account and data, even without the app, at https://112103.github.io/ipo-radar/delete-account/

11. Deletion request form

If you use the request form on our website, we receive the name, email address and message you enter, by email (sent through the FormSubmit service, formsubmit.co). We use them only to handle your request and reply to you, and delete them within 30 days after your request is completed.

12. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, protected by your phone's own security. All network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Please use a screen lock. No method of storage is completely secure.

13. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws you may access, correct and erase your data. Because your data is on your phone, you can do this directly in the app. For anything else, email us.

14. Children

The app is meant for people aged 18 and above who can invest in IPOs. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

15. Other websites

Buttons like Apply, View application and registrar links open other websites and apps (brokers, NSE, BSE, registrars). Their privacy policies apply there.

16. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above will change and important changes will be shown in the app. Questions, requests or complaints (grievances): frontend.divine@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 days.
