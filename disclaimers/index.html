Information in this app is for education and convenience only. It is not a recommendation to apply for, buy or sell any IPO or share. We are not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst.

Grey market premium (GMP)

GMP is an unofficial, unregulated figure collected from informal sources. It changes often, can be manipulated, and is not a guaranteed or expected listing price. "Estimated listing" values are simple calculations from GMP, not predictions. Grey market trading is not recognised by SEBI or the stock exchanges.

Data accuracy

IPO dates, prices, lot sizes, subscription and other details come from third-party sources and may be delayed or wrong. Confirm on NSE, BSE, the registrar or the offer document.
